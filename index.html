How to play

You’re a spoiled heiress with way too much stuff. Get rid of it — in style.

1

Grab

Walk up to luxury items and pick them up. Your hands only fit a few, so choose wisely.

2

Toss

Drop them into the vortex and watch them turn into coins, bills and gold bars. Chain drops to build a combo.

3

Beat the quota

Hit the score quota before the 30-second clock runs out. Fulfil Today’s Whims for bonus points and extra time.

4

Shop & risk it

Spend your cash at the boutique, pick a risky contract, and survive the Gala boss every five rounds.

Round→Boutique→Contract→Round→…→GALA NIGHT

Meet the cast

One heiress. Lots of cameras. Absolutely no privacy.

The Heiress

Rich, bored and carrying far too much. Fast on her designer sneakers.

You

Paparazzi

They chase you from round 3. One flash and you drop everything you’re carrying.

Danger

Supreme Paparazzi

The Gala boss. Fires flashbulbs, charges at you, and only whims can hurt him.

Boss

Enraged!

At half health he goes berserk: faster, angrier, and firing in a fan of three.

Phase 2

Champagne puddles

Sticky, bubbly and slow. They’re everywhere from round 2.

Hazard

Spikes

They blink orange, then pop out. Don’t be standing there.

Hazard

Luxury in. Cash out.

Twelve items worth one to ten points each. Legendary ones glow. Everything you toss comes back as money.

Every run is different

You can’t buy everything. Pick your build, accept the risk, and see how far the fortune goes.

Rotating boutique

Five offers per visit: upgrades, consumables and relics. Prices climb every round, and rerolling costs more each time.

Choose wisely

Relics with a catch

Double your cash… but the paparazzi run 30% faster. Every relic has an upside and a downside.

8 relics

Risk contracts

Before each round pick calm, or gamble: Double or Nothing, Shopping Rush, VIP Paparazzi, Luxury Quota, Champagne Floor.

High risk, high cash

Today’s Whims

Deliver the exact items on the heiress’s wish list for big bonuses, extra seconds, and damage to the boss.

Combo it up

A restless vortex

From round 2 the vortex warns you, then jumps across the map or drifts away. Never get comfortable.

Keep moving

Gala night

Every fifth round is a Gala. Beat the Supreme Paparazzi to earn a free relic at the boutique.
